# Week 1: Day 1 - Salesforce & CRM Basics

## 1. What is CRM?
**CRM (Customer Relationship Management)** is a technology used to manage all your company’s relationships and interactions with customers and potential customers. 

Instead of using messy spreadsheets, a CRM centralizes data so teams can track communications, sales, and service history in one place.

---

## 2. Why Companies Use Salesforce
*   **Cloud-Based:** No need to install expensive hardware; access it anywhere.
*   **Scalability:** It grows with the business, from startups to global enterprises.
*   **Customization:** You can build custom apps and workflows without heavy coding.
*   **AppExchange:** A massive marketplace for pre-built apps to extend functionality.

---

## 3. Core Salesforce Objects
*   **Account:** A company or organization you do business with (The "Who").
*   **Contact:** An individual person associated with an Account.
*   **Opportunity:** A potential deal or sale in progress that you are tracking.

---

## 4. Real-World Mapping: (College Admission)


| Salesforce Object | College Admission Equivalent | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Account** | High School / School District | The institution the student is coming from. |
| **Contact** | Student (Applicant) | The individual person applying for a degree. |
| **Opportunity** | Application | The specific "deal"—tracking the student from "Applied" to "Enrolled." |
| **Lead** | Prospective Student | Someone who filled out an inquiry form but hasn't applied yet. |
